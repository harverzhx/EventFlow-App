## Screen 1 — Login

> ``When the user opens the app, the first thing they see is the EventFlow brand mark › a rounded square app logo with a white event-flow glyph › glowing softly at the top center of the screen under a gentle white-to-blue aurora backdrop. Just below it, the wordmark "EventFlow" appears with "Event" in deep navy and "Flow" in bright brand blue, and under that, the headline "Welcome back". A short muted line underneath reads "Sign in to continue to your EventFlow dashboard." In the middle of the screen sits a frosted-glass card with rounded 20-pixel corners. Inside the card are two labeled fields: "Email / Username" with a mail icon and the grey hint text "you@example.com", then "Password" with a lock icon, a hide/show eye toggle, and the grey hint text "Enter your password". A brand-blue link, "Forgot password?", is aligned to the right above a full-width blue gradient button labeled "Login" with a log-in arrow icon. At the very bottom of the screen, centered and in muted text, the app asks "Don't have an account?" followed by the bold blue link "Sign Up."``

**Short version:** Sign in to continue to your EventFlow dashboard. Displays the logo, wordmark, "Welcome back" heading, Email/Username and Password fields, Login button, Forgot password link, and Sign Up link on a glass card over an aurora backdrop.

## Screen 2 — Sign Up

> ``Tapping Sign Up slides the user to the registration screen. A circular frosted back arrow sits at the top left corner. At the top of a frosted-glass card, the heading "Create your account" appears in navy, with the muted subtitle "Start planning and managing your events in minutes." Four fields follow briskly: "Full Name" with a user icon and the hint "Juan Dela Cruz"; "Email" with a mail icon and the hint "you@example.com"; "Password" with a lock icon, a show/hide toggle, and the hint "Create a password"; and "Confirm Password" with a check icon and the hint "Re-enter your password". If the two passwords do not match, the app shows a red error line under the Confirm field that says "Passwords do not match." and the field border turns red. Below the fields, a full-width blue gradient button labeled "Sign Up" with a person-plus icon submits the form. At the bottom of the screen the muted text "Already have an account?" is followed by the bold blue link "Login."``

**Short version:** Start planning and managing your events in minutes. Shows the back arrow, "Create your account" heading, Full Name, Email, Password, and Confirm Password fields with live password-match validation, the Sign Up button, and the Login link.
